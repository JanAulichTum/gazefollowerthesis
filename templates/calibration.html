Eye-Tracker Setup

Calibration and a short accuracy check before the videos begin

Please set up your environment first:

  • Place the laptop on a table (not your lap), webcam at eye level.
  • Sit about {{ viewing_distance_cm|int }} cm from the screen (roughly arm's length).
  • Light your face from the front; avoid windows or lamps behind you.
  • If possible, remove glasses with strong reflections.
  • Keep your head still during calibration and the videos — move only your eyes.

Clicking the button opens a fullscreen calibration: simply look at each dot as it appears. Afterwards a required accuracy check measures how precisely your gaze is tracked — the result is stored with your session.

For the most accurate calibration

  1. Camera at eye level. This is the single most important step. A webcam below eye level makes the tracker read your gaze too high when you look up. Raise the laptop (a stack of books works) until the camera is level with your eyes.
  2. Look at the exact centre of each dot and hold until it moves on — don't glance ahead to the next one.
  3. Give the top dots special attention. The upper edge is the hardest for a webcam; really fixate the dots near the top.
  4. Move only your eyes, keep your head still — during calibration and the videos.
  5. Even light from the front, no window or lamp behind you; minimise glasses glare if you can.
